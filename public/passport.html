Questions
What is a Passport?
A Passport is a one-time $11 payment that gives you permanent access to the GFS group chat, all artist feeds, and the full music library. No recurring charges.
What is the difference between Passport and All Access?
Passport unlocks everything for a single payment. All Access adds monthly exclusive drops and locked tracks for $11/month -- think of it as staying at the front of the line.
Can I cancel All Access?
Yes -- cancel any time from your account. Your Passport access (chat + library) stays active even after you cancel All Access, as long as you originally purchased a Passport separately.
What happens after I pay?
You are redirected to the GFS welcome page with your login details. Access is instant. The chat, music, and artist feeds are live the moment your payment clears.
Is there a free option?
You can preview two 30-second sample tracks and browse the public artist pages without a Passport. The group chat, full library, and drops require membership.
