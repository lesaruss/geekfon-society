Questions
How does Passport work?
Passport is $11 per artist, paid once. You get lifetime access to that artist's current tracks and every future release they put on GFS. Pick as many artists as you want at checkout, or come back and add more later.
Can I add more artists later?
Yes. Come back to this page and select any artists you have not unlocked yet. Each new artist is $11 and adds to your existing access.
What is the difference between Passport and All Access?
Passport is $11 once per artist -- lifetime access to their current and future tracks. All Access is $11/month and covers every artist on the platform. Lifetime is $111 once and covers every current and future artist, with early access to new tracks before All Access members hear them. Lifetime sunsets July 1, 2026.
What happens after I pay?
You land on the GFS welcome page with your login details. Access to the artists you selected is live immediately.
Is there a free option?
You can preview two 30-second sample tracks and browse public artist pages without a Passport. The group chat, full library, and drops require membership.
